About this preview

This is an early website. It reads the checked deals in the repository. The email stays in this browser. It is not sent to a server. A result names your school and how each deal was checked. The brand makes the final check.

PerkCheck is free. A paid link is always labeled. Logos are shown only to identify each deal and belong to their owners. No endorsement is implied.
